# English Engine Data License

## `Sources/EnglishEngine/Resources/wordlist.txt`

The English completion lexicon is generated by `scripts/english/build-wordlist.py`
from the word frequency data in [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq)
3.1.1 by Robyn Speer.

- The word list is a derivative of wordfreq's data and is licensed under
  [CC-BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- It keeps the top 30,000 lowercase `[a-z]+` English words in frequency order,
  and drops profanity and slurs so they are never offered as completions.
- This license applies only to the data file, not to the project source code.

### Upstream attribution

wordfreq's data is built from the following sources:

- Google Books Ngrams (<http://books.google.com/ngrams>) and Google Books
  Syntactic Ngrams
- The Leeds Internet Corpus, University of Leeds Centre for Translation Studies
- Wikipedia (<http://www.wikipedia.org>)
- ParaCrawl (<https://paracrawl.eu>)
- OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 (<http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php>), with data
  originating from OpenSubtitles (<http://www.opensubtitles.org/>)
- SUBTLEX word lists by Marc Brysbaert et al., freely available at
  <http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies>
- Word statistics collected from the Twitter streaming API

Citation: Robyn Speer. (2022). rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0. Zenodo.

## `Sources/EnglishEngine/Resources/zh-en.tsv`

The Chinese-to-English translation table is generated by
`scripts/english/build-translations.py` from
[CC-CEDICT](https://cc-cedict.org/) (release 2026-09-26, distributed by
[MDBG](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict)).

- The table is a derivative of CC-CEDICT and is licensed under
  [CC-BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- It keeps simplified headwords of two or more characters with at most two
  cleaned English glosses each; cross-references, classifiers, and long
  descriptions are removed.
- This license applies only to the data file, not to the project source code.

CC-CEDICT is maintained by the CC-CEDICT community and is based on the original
CEDICT by Paul Andrew Denisowski.
